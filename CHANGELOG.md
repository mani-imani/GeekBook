# Changelog

## v1.0.2 · Nov 2026
- Stats: in "reading days" and "pages per month" time now runs left to right (older on the left, today and this month on the right).
- Reading-days map: the whole year fits on wide screens; on phones only whole weeks are shown, so no column is cut in half at an edge; the frame around today is no longer clipped; days still to come are no longer drawn; month names no longer overlap.
- Quote cards: Persian quotes are justified like a printed book (the last line of each paragraph stays as it is). When a size would leave wide gaps or one word alone on the last line, a slightly smaller size is used. English quotes are unchanged.

## v1.0.1 · Nov 2026
- Empty text fields are right-to-left: the Persian example text and the caret used to sit on the left side. Once you type, the field still follows the direction of your text, so a Latin title reads correctly.

## v1.0.0 · Nov 2026
- First version: library with typed-in book details and colored text covers (no cover images), five statuses (reading, unread, read, dropped, to buy), "where I stopped" with page and a one-line note, page-based progress with an estimated finish, automatic start and finish dates.
- To-buy list with price and priority, custom lists, lending log.
- Reading entries with a short summary from memory, quotes with page numbers, spaced review (1 day, 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months), rating and a short review.
- Streak, Jalali heatmap of reading days, daily and yearly goals, stats page, year recap.
- Share images for quotes and the year recap, Markdown export per book, JSON backup and restore.
- A short guide to reading so that it stays.
- Installable, fully offline, Persian and right-to-left, in the GeekMani product family design.
